PART A — READING (70 MARKS)

1-2. Read the passage and answer MCQ (7) and short questions (10):
Sadia gets up early and drinks a glass of water before breakfast. She walks to school with her cousin because the road is safe and the distance is short. At school she plays games during the break. Her mother packs fruit and homemade bread for lunch. In the afternoon, Sadia finishes her homework and spends some time outdoors. She knows that healthy food, physical activity and enough sleep help children stay active and cheerful.

The market helps families buy fresh food and supports local workers.
