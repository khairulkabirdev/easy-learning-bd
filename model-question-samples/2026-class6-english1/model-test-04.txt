PART A — READING (70 MARKS)

1-2. Read the passage and answer MCQ (7) and short questions (10):
Rafi's school has a small digital learning room. Once a week, students visit the room with their English teacher. They listen to short recordings, read passages on a large screen and practise speaking in pairs. Before using a computer, they wash their hands and follow the teacher's instructions. Rafi enjoys the lessons because he can hear the correct pronunciation of new words. He has learned that technology is most useful when students use it wisely.

1. MCQ — Choose the correct answer (1×7=7):

  What does the school have?
  a) A swimming pool | b) A digital learning room | c) A new market | d) A train

  How often do students use the room?
  a) Once a week | b) Daily at night | c) Once a year | d) Never

  Which teacher goes with them?
  a) Science | b) Art | c) English | d) Mathematics

  What do students practise in pairs?
  a) Running | b) Speaking | c) Fishing | d) Cooking

  What do students do before using computers?
  a) Eat snacks | b) Wash their hands | c) Go home | d) Switch off lights

  What does Rafi learn from recordings?
  a) Pronunciation | b) Gardening | c) Painting | d) Driving

  When is technology most useful?
  a) When used wisely | b) When used all night | c) When used without rules | d) When it replaces reading

  5. What is the main message?

3-4. Read Passage 2 and answer Gap Filling and Vocabulary:
Salma's family makes handwoven baskets in a small village. They use bamboo and natural fibres, and each basket takes several hours to finish. Salma helps by sorting the materials and checking the shapes. Her parents sell the finished baskets at a nearby fair. Visitors appreciate the strong, beautiful designs. The work helps the family earn money while keeping a traditional skill alive.

Every Tuesday, community health worker Farzana visits families in a rural area. She talks to parents about clean drinking water, handwashing and nutritious food. This month, she visited forty homes. At the community hall on Friday, she organised a health awareness meeting for twenty parents. A local nurse answered questions about common illnesses and explained when families should visit a clinic. Everyone received a simple health information leaflet.
