PART A — READING (70 MARKS)

1-2. Read the passage and answer MCQ (7) and short questions (10):
Every Thursday, students at Green Field School meet after class to care for their small garden. Their teacher, Ms Rahman, helps them plant vegetables and flowers. Rina records how much water the plants need, while Fahim collects dry leaves for compost. At first, only eight students joined the club. Soon their friends noticed the clean garden and wanted to help. The members now share their vegetables with the school kitchen. They have learned that even a small project can improve a community.

1. MCQ — Choose the correct answer (1×7=7):

  When do the students meet?
  a) Every Monday | b) Every Thursday | c) Every Saturday | d) Every Sunday

  What do the students care for?
  a) A playground | b) A school garden | c) A fish pond | d) A library

  Who guides the club?
  a) Rina | b) Fahim | c) Ms Rahman | d) The head cook

  What does Rina record?
  a) Book names | b) Water needs | c) Bus times | d) Attendance at games

  What does Fahim collect?
  a) Dry leaves | b) Old toys | c) Plastic bottles | d) Fruits

  How many students joined at first?
  a) Five | b) Six | c) Eight | d) Ten

  What do the members do with the vegetables?
  a) Sell them abroad | b) Throw them away | c) Share with the school kitchen | d) Keep them all

2. Answering Questions (2×5=10):

  1. Where is the garden?

  2. Why does Fahim collect dry leaves?

  3. What encouraged more students to join?

  4. Who helps the students plant?

  5. What lesson did the club learn?

3-4. Read Passage 2 and answer Gap Filling and Vocabulary:
Maya visits the village library twice a week. The building is modest, but its shelves hold many useful books. The librarian welcomes children and encourages them to ask questions. Maya enjoys stories about birds, rivers and brave people. She sometimes borrows a science book to learn new facts. Before leaving, she returns each book to its proper place. Her careful habit helps other readers find the books they need.

3. Gap Filling (1×5=5):

5-7. Read the Unseen Passage and answer Information Transfer, True / False, and Summary:
On Monday morning, a community centre organised a safety workshop for sixty children. The programme started at nine o'clock. A volunteer named Imran explained how to read weather warnings and where to find safe shelter during heavy rain. Each child received a small information card. At noon, the group practised an evacuation route with their teachers. The workshop ended at one o'clock, and everyone thanked the volunteers.

10. Answer any five questions from the original poem below (2×5=10):
