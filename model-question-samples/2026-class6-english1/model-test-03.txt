PART A — READING (70 MARKS)

1-2. Read the passage and answer MCQ (7) and short questions (10):
During the winter holidays, Karim visited his grandmother in a riverside village. Early one morning, they travelled by boat to a nearby market. Karim saw fishermen repairing nets and birds resting on the riverbank. His grandmother explained that many families depend on the river for work and transport. She also warned him not to throw rubbish into the water. Karim returned home with a new respect for the river and the people who care for it.

  How did they travel to market?
  a) By train | b) By bus | c) By boat | d) By bicycle

  What were fishermen repairing?

At the village fair, Nila watched an artist make colourful clay pots. The artist shaped wet clay with careful hands and placed the pots in the sun to dry. Nila learned that pottery is a traditional craft in Bangladesh. People use pots for cooking, storing water and decorating homes. The artist said that practice and patience are important for good work. Nila bought a small pot as a gift for her mother.

The students of River View School started a recycling project in September. Their classroom teacher, Ms Akter, placed three labelled bins near the gate: one for paper, one for plastic and one for metal. Twenty-five students collected clean materials every Wednesday. Once a month, volunteers took the materials to a recycling centre. The school used the money earned to buy new storybooks for its library.
